Anatomical surface: brain-surface.obj.gz, from BrainBrowser (aces/brainbrowser).
Source: https://github.com/aces/brainbrowser/blob/master/examples/models/brain-surface.obj.gz
Project: https://brainbrowser.cbrain.mcgill.ca/
Repository license: GNU Affero General Public License v3 (included as LICENSE.txt).
Original compressed mesh is retained alongside the derived cortex.bin.
Adaptations: centered, uniformly scaled, axes rotated, split by midline into two
render groups, and encoded as little-endian indexed geometry. Geometry is not a
clinical model. Conversion source: scripts/prepare-cortex.py in this project.
